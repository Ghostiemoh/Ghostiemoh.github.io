💡 What: Added semantic `aria-label`s to the icon-only social media links and mobile menu toggle buttons. Added `aria-expanded` to the mobile menu open button. Also added the `rel="noopener noreferrer"` attribute to the external social links for security.
🎯 Why: Without ARIA labels, screen reader users encounter unlabelled icon links/buttons with no context as to their function. By explicitly setting their functions with `aria-label` and `aria-expanded`, the components become fully accessible.
